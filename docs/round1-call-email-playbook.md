# iotty Trade Partners — Round 1 Outreach Playbook (Calls + Email)

**Companion to:** Trade Program Pilot Plan (§7)
**Motion:** phone and email run together — the email earns the call a reason, the call earns the email a reply.
**Round 1 unit:** the Hermes prospecting stack feeds **50 new profiled contacts per day** into the shared database (see Pilot Plan §8). The email side of the cadence runs automatically for every contact; the **daily call queue is the top ~15–20 by fit score** who are due for a phone touch — phone time is the scarce resource, so the score decides who gets a human.

---

## 1. The 14-Day Cadence (8 touches)

| Day | Touch | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 AM | **Email 1** — intro + Founding Partner offer | Short; subject references their trade + "50 founding slots" |
| 1 PM | **Call 1 + Voicemail A** | "Calling behind an email I sent this morning" — the email makes the call warm |
| 3 | **Call 2** (no voicemail) + LinkedIn connect | Different time-of-day than Call 1 |
| 5 | **Email 2** — the margin math | One table/image: "same 15 minutes of labor, 4× the ticket" |
| 8 | **Call 3 + Voicemail B** | Demo-kit hook: "your first glass switch is on us" |
| 11 | **Email 3** — proof + scarcity | Partner spotlight/install photo + live founding-slot count |
| 14 | **Call 4**, then **Email 4** (breakup) | "Closing the file / releasing the slot" — politely final |

No response after Day 14 → monthly nurture list (program updates, install photos). Any inbound reply or connect at any point jumps straight to the 15-minute welcome/qualify call.

**Call windows that fit the trades:** electricians and remodelers answer **6:30–8:00 AM** (truck time, before the site) and **3:30–5:30 PM** (wrap-up) local time; integrators and design/build offices connect best **10 AM–12 PM**; designers late morning or **1–3 PM**. National pilot = work the time zones east→west so every block hits someone's window.

**Daily operating rhythm (one person, ~2 hrs of dials):** two 45–60 min call blocks in the AM/PM windows, emails and LinkedIn between blocks. At typical trade connect rates (15–20%), a 60-dial day yields 9–12 live conversations.

## 2. Call 1 Script — Core Frame (all segments)

**Opener (permission-based, ~20 seconds):**

> "Hey, is this {first name}? {First name}, it's Ryan with iotty Trade Partners — you may have seen my email this morning. I know I'm catching you cold, can I take 30 seconds and you can tell me if it's relevant?"

*(Yes / go ahead)*

> "We make the Italian tempered-glass smart switches — the backlit touch panels. We're opening our US trade program and we're holding **50 founding spots nationally**: 35% off MSRP locked for 12 months, a listing on our installer directory, and your first switch free with the demo kit. I'm calling {trade}s in {state} first. Fair to ask — do you touch residential jobs where the client would pay for a premium switch?"

**From there, two qualifying questions max:**
1. "What's a typical job for you — renos, new builds, service work?"
2. "When a client asks for smart switches today, what do you put in?"

**The close (always the same):** book the 15-minute welcome call *or* take the application live.

> "Easiest next step — the application's 5 minutes at iottytradepartners.com, and I approve founding partners within a day. Want me to hold a slot and text you the link while we're talking?"

**Rule:** never pitch specs on the phone; pitch the *program* (margin, directory, founding status). The demo kit sells the product later — in their hands.

## 3. Segment Variants (swap into the core frame)

**Electricians / electrical contractors** — lead with ticket size:
> "Straight math: a 2-gang runs $130 retail, your founding price is $84.50. Same box, same 15 minutes of wire time as the $25 Decora you're installing now — but you're billing a premium ticket the customer shows their friends."

**Custom integrators (AV/smart home)** — lead with the walkthrough:
> "You've had the client walkthrough where the $200k system ends at a plastic rocker. This is the switch that matches the rest of the job — glass, backlit, no hub, works with Alexa, Google Home, Siri Shortcuts, and SmartThings. Founding dealers get it at 35 off and a featured directory listing."

**Builders / remodelers** — lead with the model home:
> "Two or three of these in the model home's kitchen and primary suite is the cheapest 'wow' on your options sheet. I'm not asking you to spec a community today — I want 20 minutes on how founding builders are packaging it as a buyer upgrade."

**Interior designers / architects** — lead with the mood board:
> "It's the one smart-home product that actually belongs on a mood board — Italian tempered glass, black or white, custom backlight. Trade pricing on client purchases, and your electrician of record gets fast-tracked into the program so install is never your problem."

## 5. Phone-Specific Objection Handling

- **"Just send me an email."** → "Already did — this morning, subject line has 'founding slots' in it. While I've got you: 20 seconds — do you touch residential jobs where a client would pay for a premium switch? If not, I'll close the file and you'll never hear from me again."
- **"We're a Lutron shop."** → "Perfect — you should stay one. This isn't a replacement, it's the premium line for the jobs where Caséta plastic doesn't match the finish budget. No certification, standard box. That's exactly who the founding spots are for."
- **"How much?" (price-first callers)** → Give it straight, then reframe: "$130 retail on the 2-gang, $84.50 your cost as a founding partner. You bill it at retail inside the job — that's ~$45 margin before labor on 15 minutes of wire time."
- **"Never heard of iotty."** → "That's honestly the opportunity — Lutron's directory has thousands of installers; ours launches with fifty. Early partners own their metro on the map. The demo kit is how you judge the product: first switch is free, install it in your own place this weekend."
- **"Call me back in the fall / it's pre-order."** → "That's exactly why I'm calling now — founding pricing exists *because* it's pre-launch. Application now locks the 35 for 12 months; you don't buy anything until you've got a job for it."
- **Hard no.** → Thank them, mark DNC, ask one exit question if the tone allows: "Fair enough — out of curiosity, what would a switch line have to offer for you to carry it?" (log the answer; it's free market research).

## 6. Email Touches (short versions — full sequences can be drafted next)

- **Email 1 (Day 1):** Subject: "50 founding slots for {state} {trade}s — iotty Trade Partners." Three sentences: what iotty is (one line + photo), the founding offer (35% locked 12 months, free first switch, directory listing), the link. P.S.: "I'll try your office this afternoon — 30 seconds is all I need."
- **Email 2 (Day 5):** Subject: "The math on a $130 switch." The margin table and the one-liner: same 15 minutes of labor, 4× the ticket. Link.
- **Email 3 (Day 11):** Subject: "{N} of 50 founding slots left." Install photo or partner spotlight, live count, link.
- **Email 4 (Day 14):** Subject: "Closing your file at iotty." Two sentences: releasing the held slot, door stays open at standard trade pricing. One link, no pitch.

## 7. Round 1 Phone KPIs (per 50-contact batch)

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Dial-to-connect rate | 15–20% | Trade norms; below 10% = bad data or wrong windows |
| Connect → real conversation | ≥60% | Opener health check |
| Conversation → application/booked call | ≥20% | Pitch health check |
| Voicemail → callback/reply | 3–5% | VMs work with the email, not alone |
| Batch yield after 14 days | 5–8 applications | With Hermes at 50/day, weekly cohorts should beat the plan's Week-6 goal of 75 applications |

Log every dial (connect y/n, disposition, objection heard) — the objection tally after 200 dials rewrites this script better than any planning session.
